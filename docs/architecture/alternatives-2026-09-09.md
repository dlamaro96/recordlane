# MDM landscape note

This is a dated positioning check, not a benchmark or procurement verdict. Vendor statements are labeled as claims; no competing product was installed or performance-tested for this assignment.

| Product | Verified source statement | Recordlane 0.1 behavior | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reltio | Reltio describes a SaaS, cloud-native multidomain service with identity resolution, dynamic survivorship, relationships, hierarchies, lineage, and governance ([official product page](https://www.reltio.com/master-data-management/)). | Apache-2.0 self-hosting, deterministic resolution, attribute provenance, governed correction, PostgreSQL deployment. | Reltio claims were not independently tested; Recordlane has no equivalent scale evidence or broad connector catalog. |
| Semarchy xDM | Semarchy documents both SaaS and self-hosted deployment choices ([official documentation hub](https://docs.semarchy.com/)); Microsoft’s architecture describes quality, matching, deduplication, authoring, curation, REST/SQL integration, and Purview lineage ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/data-governance-master-data-management-semarchy)). | Self-hosted modular monolith with REST contracts and editable evidence diagrams. | No feature/performance equivalence claim; Semarchy was not installed. |
| Ataccama ONE MDM | Ataccama documents a metadata-driven MDM server and web application with corrections, authoring, merge/split, quality tasks, configuration, security, audit, and APIs ([official MDM docs](https://docs.ataccama.com/mdm/latest/overview.html)). | Working local mastering/governance flows with version-bound approvals, hard constraints, and open deployment packaging. | Ataccama was not installed; its documented feature breadth exceeds this alpha in several areas. |
| Pimcore | Pimcore documents structured data objects and configurable models for PIM/MDM ([official overview](https://docs.pimcore.com/platform/2025.4/Pimcore/Overview/)). Current edition documentation calls the platform open-core under POCL and describes eligibility constraints for Community ([official editions page](https://docs.pimcore.com/platform/2026.1/Pimcore_Platform/Pimcore_Editions/)). | Recordlane core is Apache-2.0 without record ceilings or paid-only auth/audit. | Product scope differs: Pimcore also covers DAM/DXP/PIM. License suitability needs independent legal review. |

Recordlane’s intended distinction is inspectable evidence and deployability: source contributions remain separate, selection is explained per attribute, unsafe clusters are constrained, correction creates repair work, and core operation has no mandatory external AI or hosted account. The current alpha does **not** claim production readiness, certified vendor connectors, competitive benchmark superiority, SCIM, or complete browser SSO.
